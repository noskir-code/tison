# Server Rules — {{INSTANCE_NAME}}

**Last updated:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Version française :** [Règles du serveur]({{FRENCH_RULES_URL}})

These rules apply to everyone with an account on {{INSTANCE_NAME}}, and to
how we moderate content arriving from other servers. They are part of our
[Terms of Use]({{TERMS_URL}}). Moderators interpret them in good faith and in
context; a behaviour does not have to be listed here to be acted upon if it
clearly goes against their spirit.

---

## Part A — The rules (short version)

1. **No hate or discrimination.** No racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia,
   ableism, antisemitism, Islamophobia, casteism, xenophobia or other
   bigotry, including "jokes", dog whistles and hateful symbols.
2. **No harassment.** No targeted attacks, dogpiling, stalking, doxxing,
   threats, or contacting people who have blocked you through other means.
3. **Nothing illegal and no sexualization of minors.** Includes drawn and
   AI-generated content. Zero tolerance; reported to authorities.
4. **Use content warnings and mark sensitive media.** Sexual content,
   gore, and the topics listed in our CW guide must be behind a CW.
5. **Respect consent and privacy.** No intimate images without consent,
   no posting private information or private messages without permission.
6. **No violent extremism or glorification of violence.** No incitement,
   terrorist propaganda, or praise of mass violence.
7. **Be honest.** No impersonation, deliberate disinformation that can
   cause real harm, or undisclosed ads. Label bots, parody and AI-generated
   media.
8. **Describe your media.** Add alt text to images and videos when you can.
9. **{{COMMUNITY_SPECIFIC_RULE}}** (e.g. "Primary language: French or
   English" / "Stay on topic: this server is about …")

*Spam, scams and malware are also forbidden and can be reported through the
built-in report form.*

## Part B — The rules explained

### 1. No hate or discrimination

Not allowed:
- Content attacking, dehumanizing or excluding people based on race,
  ethnicity, national origin, caste, religion, sex, gender, gender identity
  or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, illness, or
  immigration status.
- Slurs used against people, hateful symbols and coded language
  ("dog whistles"), and "just asking questions" or "debate" framings used to
  deny people's identity, rights or existence (e.g. misgendering or
  deadnaming someone on purpose, Holocaust denial).
- Promotion of hate groups or ideologies (white supremacy, neo-Nazism, and
  similar).

Allowed: reclaimed language used by members of the community concerned;
reporting on, documenting or critiquing hate, with context and a content
warning where needed; criticism of ideas, institutions and governments.

### 2. No harassment

Not allowed:
- Repeatedly replying to, mentioning or tagging someone who has shown they
  do not want to interact with you.
- Organizing or joining pile-ons ("dogpiling"), including quote-posting or
  screenshotting someone to direct your followers at them.
- Threats of any kind, including "joking" threats and wishing harm.
- **Doxxing:** publishing a person's real name, address, workplace,
  phone number or other identifying details they have not made public
  themselves.
- Creating new accounts to contact people who blocked you, or evading a
  suspension.
- Stalking, including following someone across servers to monitor them.

Disagreement and criticism are not harassment. Persistence after being asked
to stop is.

### 3. Nothing illegal; no sexualization of minors

- Content that sexualizes minors, real or fictional, including drawings,
  "loli/shota" art, and AI-generated images, is banned without exception.
  Accounts are suspended immediately, content is preserved as the law
  requires, and it is reported to Cybertip.ca or the police.
- No content that is illegal in Canada, including the sale of illegal
  goods, fraud, and copyright infringement (see Terms, section 7).
- Sexual content must not involve anyone who has not consented to it
  appearing.

### 4. Content warnings (CW) and sensitive media

Put the following behind a content warning, with a clear description
(e.g. "CW: violence, war news"), and mark images as sensitive where it
applies:
- Sexual or explicit content (allowed: {{NSFW_POLICY: "yes, if marked
  sensitive and behind a CW" / "no, this is a SFW server"}});
- Gore, injury, and graphic violence;
- Self-harm, suicide, and eating disorders;
- {{CW_TOPICS: e.g. "Detailed discussion of abuse, violent news, phobias
  (spiders, needles), flashing images"}}.

Optional but appreciated: CWs for long posts, spoilers, politics, and food.
Mark media that could trigger photosensitive epilepsy (flashing).

**Avatars and headers** must be safe for work, because they cannot be hidden.

### 5. Consent and privacy

- Do not post intimate or sexual images of anyone without their
  consent, including deepfakes (this is also a crime in Canada).
- Do not share screenshots of direct messages or of content from locked /
  followers-only accounts without permission, unless it is necessary to
  document abuse to moderators.
- Ask before posting identifiable photos of people in private settings,
  and especially of children.

### 6. Violent extremism

No content that incites violence, celebrates terrorist attacks or mass
killings, recruits for violent groups, or shares their propaganda. News
reporting and analysis are allowed with context and a CW.

### 7. Honesty and authenticity

- **Impersonation:** do not pretend to be another person or organization.
  Parody and fan accounts must say so in their name or bio.
- **Disinformation:** do not knowingly spread false information likely
  to cause real harm, such as dangerous medical claims, election
  disinformation (wrong voting dates, places or procedures), or fake
  emergency alerts.
- **Advertising:** occasional self-promotion of your own work is fine.
  Accounts mainly used for advertising, affiliate links, or undisclosed
  sponsored posts are not.
- **Bots and automation:** automated accounts must be marked as bots in
  their profile, must not post more than {{BOT_RATE_LIMIT}} public posts
  per hour, should use "unlisted" visibility by default, must only reply to
  people who interact with them, and must have a human contact listed in
  the bio.
- **AI-generated media:** label AI-generated images, audio and video
  (e.g. in the alt text or with a hashtag such as #AIart).

### 8. Accessibility

Add descriptions (alt text) to images and videos, so blind and visually
impaired people can follow. Use CamelCase in multi-word hashtags
(#ThisIsReadable) so screen readers pronounce them correctly. Not a strict
requirement, but moderators may remind you.

## Enforcement

Moderators choose a proportionate response depending on severity, intent
and history:

| Level | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Reminder / warning | First minor issue (e.g. missing CW) |
| Content marked sensitive, CW added, or removed | Content that breaks a rule |
| Limited (silenced) account | Repeated issues; posts only visible to followers |
| Temporary freeze | Ongoing harmful behaviour during an investigation |
| Suspension | Serious or repeated violations; rules 1, 2, 3 or 6 |

Rule 3 (sexualization of minors) and credible threats lead to immediate
suspension without warning.

You will be notified of actions on your account and can appeal as described
in section 6 of the [Terms of Use]({{TERMS_URL}}).

**Reporting:** use the "Report" button on a post or profile, or email
{{MODERATION_EMAIL}}. Reports are confidential. Remote users can report our
members through their own server.

## Part C — Our commitments as operators

We commit to:

1. **Active moderation** against racism, sexism, homophobia,
   transphobia and other forms of hate, and to answering reports
   within {{REPORT_RESPONSE_TIME}} (target: 48 hours).
2. **Daily backups**, encrypted and stored in a separate location.
3. **At least one other trusted person with emergency access** to the
   server infrastructure.
4. **At least 3 months' notice** before shutting down the server, with
   data export and account migration kept available until the end.
5. **No advertising, no data sale, no AI training** on your content
   (see [Privacy Policy]({{PRIVACY_URL}})).
6. **Transparency:** publishing who our moderators are
   ({{MODERATORS_LIST}}), our list of moderated servers, and any change to
   these rules.

## Part D — Federation policy

We aim to federate openly. We will **limit** (silence) or **suspend**
(defederate from) another server when it:
- hosts or tolerates content that breaks rules 1, 3 or 6;
- is a source of harassment or spam that its staff will not address;
- is run with no moderation, or primarily to harass or surveil;
- federates illegal content, or poses a technical threat.

We may also reject media from servers that host a lot of content we cannot
accept but whose text content is fine.

Decisions are made by {{FEDERATION_DECISION_PROCESS: e.g. "the moderation
team, by consensus"}}. We consult established shared blocklists as input
({{BLOCKLIST_SOURCES}}) but review entries rather than applying them blindly.
The list of limited and suspended servers, with reasons where it is safe to
share them, is published at {{BLOCKLIST_URL}}. Remote server admins may ask
for a review at {{MODERATION_EMAIL}}.
